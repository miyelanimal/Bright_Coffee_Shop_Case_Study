Bright Coffee Shop Performance Evaluation

An end to end business intelligence project that analyzes Bright Coffee Shop's sales performance across stores, product categories, customer spend behavior, and date time patterns. The project turns raw transactional data into executive ready insights, recommendations, and a roadmap for real time analytics.

Executive takeaway:
Bright Coffee Shop is growing well, but the next phase of growth depends on shifting from volume driven sales to value driven growth by increasing basket size, improving underperforming periods, and scaling what works across stores.

---

Project Overview
This project evaluates Bright Coffee Shop's first half performance using transaction level sales data from three stores. The analysis focuses on regional performance, product category contribution, top selling products, customer spending behavior, and time based revenue patterns.

Business Problem
Bright Coffee Shop shows healthy revenue growth, but the growth is concentrated in a few strong areas. Morning hours contribute a large share of revenue, coffee and tea dominate category performance, and most transactions are still low spend purchases.

Project Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales performance
- Identify revenue drivers
- Understand customer behavior
- Provide actionable recommendations
- Propose real time analytics enhancement

Dataset Overview
Includes transaction dates, store locations, product categories, sales volume, and revenue.

Tools and Technologies
- Databricks
- Excel
- Google Looker Studio
- Canva
- GitHub

Key Business Insights
- Revenue increased by 50% from January to June
- Hell's Kitchen leads performance
- Coffee contributes 39% of revenue
- 99% of transactions are low spend
- Morning peak drives most revenue

Strategic Recommendations
- Increase spend per customer
- Replicate high performing store strategies
- Improve underperforming products
- Expand off peak sales

Real Time Analytics Roadmap
- Real time dashboards
- Alerts and notifications
- Inventory integration

Conclusion
The business is performing well, but growth can be accelerated through better spend optimization and real time decision making.
